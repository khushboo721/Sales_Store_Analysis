# Sales_Store_Analysis_Excel_SQL

## Overview  
This project analyzes a **retail store’s daily sales transactions** to uncover insights on customer behavior, product performance, and revenue trends.  
Using **Excel for data cleaning** and **SQL for querying and analysis**, the goal was to identify key sales patterns, top-performing products, and areas to optimize operations and improve profitability.

##  Business Background  
A retail store chain tracks sales, customers, orders, and product details across multiple categories.  
However, the business lacked visibility into:  
- Which products or categories drive the most revenue  
- Which customers spend the most  
- What times of day see peak transactions  
- Why certain products face frequent returns or cancellations  

These insights were crucial for **increasing sales, improving service quality, and reducing operational inefficiencies**.

## Tools & Technologies  

 **Microsoft Excel** - Cleaned and validated raw data (handled duplicates, missing values, and formatting). 
 **SQL Server** - Stored structured data, executed analytical queries for sales, customer, and product insights. 
  
##  Business Questions Answered  
1️⃣ What are the top 5 most sold products by quantity?  
2️⃣ Which products are most frequently canceled or returned?  
3️⃣ What time of day records the highest number of purchases?  
4️⃣ Who are the top 5 highest-spending customers?  
5️⃣ Which product categories generate the most revenue?  
6️⃣ What is the return or cancellation rate per category?  
7️⃣ Which payment mode is most preferred by customers?  
8️⃣ How do different age groups influence purchasing behavior?  
9️⃣ What are the monthly or seasonal sales trends?  
🔟 Are there any gender-based preferences in product categories?

## Key Insights  
- **Top-Selling Products:** [e.g., Smartphones, Laptops, and Headphones led total quantity sold.]  
- **Revenue Drivers:** Electronics category contributed the highest sales share.  
- **Peak Shopping Hours:** Maximum orders occurred between *6 PM – 9 PM*, indicating evening shopping preference.  
- **Top Customers:** A small group of repeat buyers contributed a significant portion of total revenue.  
- **Returns/Cancellations:** Fashion and Apparel categories had higher cancellation rates.  
- **Preferred Payment Mode:** Majority of customers paid via *Credit Card* or *UPI*.  
- **Monthly Trends:** Highest sales during *November–December* period due to festive demand.  
- **Customer Insights:** Younger age groups (18–30) showed more frequent purchases in tech and apparel segments.

##  Business Impact  
- Helped identify **top-performing products and customers** for targeted marketing.  
- Enabled **inventory optimization** by understanding demand patterns.  
- Improved **revenue forecasting** and **promotion planning** based on sales trends.
